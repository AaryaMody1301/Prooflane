# Final account and submission checklist

Target: 12 November 2026, 8 PM IST. Official cutoff: 13 November 2026, 1:30 AM IST.

- [ ] Read `FINAL_REPORT.md` and choose the tested release commit. Merge the passing PR only after review; no merge is implied by a local commit.
- [ ] Review current screenshots and finished MP4. Confirm captions match the actual modes and any separate transactions are labeled; keep secrets and private account data out.
- [ ] Upload the reviewed MP4 to YouTube as **Public**, not private or unlisted. Use `VIDEO_DESCRIPTION.txt`. Confirm playback and duration while signed out.
- [ ] In the authenticated [hackathon page](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/), check whether an existing draft already exists before choosing Start project or Import from portfolio.
- [ ] Confirm entrant eligibility, all team members and representative authority. Do not add inferred teammates or make unsupported declarations.
- [ ] Use `PROJECT_FIELDS.json` for title/tagline/tags/repository; paste `DEVPOST_STORY.md` into the story and `JUDGE_GUIDE.md` into testing instructions where the form permits.
- [ ] Add the actual public YouTube URL and current screenshot/thumbnail assets. Complete any event-specific questions visible in the real form; those were not accessible here.
- [ ] Review the completed entry and obtain the user's explicit confirmation before **Submit project**. Saving a draft is insufficient.
- [ ] After authorized submission, verify Devpost's confirmation and **Submitted** label under My projects; open the public project URL signed out.
- [ ] Save the public URLs and confirmation evidence. Replace null URLs in `PROJECT_FIELDS.json`, link them prominently in README, and update `FINAL_REPORT.md` with actual publication/submission status.
- [ ] Retain repository, instructions, video and optional working provider access through 15 December. Judges should use their own sandbox credentials; never publish yours.

Devpost's [official guide](https://help.devpost.com/article/122-how-to-enter-a-submission) explains draft/submitted states and the confirmation indicators. No email or message to organizers is sent by this checklist.
